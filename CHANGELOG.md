# Changelog

Short notes for each version. The newest release's page has the install steps.

## 5.0 (beta)

**Next (beta.48, not released yet)**
- Pair your phone with your computer by scanning a QR code and send songs and folders either way, over your own
  Wi-Fi only, encrypted, with your OK for each transfer.
- Zoom applies instantly (Ctrl + / Ctrl − / Ctrl 0).
- The equaliser's sliders now match the frequencies they change.
- Taskbar buttons on Windows (Like, Previous, Play/Pause, Next); a floating mini video player for video podcasts;
  scroll through a playlist's previews one song at a time.
- Artist pages for artists found on SoundCloud, with their albums.
- "Made for you" mixes and charts for your country, worked out on your device.
- Smooth scrolling, Help → FAQ, playlist descriptions, and many smaller fixes.
- Android: Your Library's grid shows everything as tiles. iPhone: a slimmer progress bar and tidier Now Playing.

**beta.47** Faster song start, artwork everywhere, SoundCloud as a source, Liked Songs, saved/downloaded marks
on songs, longer radios, drag folders in to make playlists.

**beta.46** Moved or deleted songs keep their details, portable mode, an activity log you can save and send,
restyled Settings, podcast filters and sorting.

**beta.37 – beta.45 (Android)** Video podcasts with a full video player (full screen, captions, speed), audio/video
switch that keeps your place, faster and more reliable playback, gapless songs.

**beta.24 – beta.36 (Android)** Smoother playback and skipping, songs found ahead of time, better search and song
radio, choose beta or stable updates, pick music folders by browsing, open/share files with Sevbify.

**beta.8 – beta.23** The Android, macOS, Linux and iPhone apps; Jellyfin and Nextcloud servers; folders from cloud
apps; Save space for podcasts and audiobooks; car support; spelling checks when importing playlists; motion and
layout polish; in-app updates on every platform.

**beta.1 – beta.7** The new 5.0 design: Home, Search, Your Library, queue, song radio, online search, playing and
saving songs you don't have yet, podcasts and audiobooks.
