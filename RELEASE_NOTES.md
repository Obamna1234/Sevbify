## What's new in beta.48

- **Pair your phone with your computer.** On the computer: Settings → Your phone → Pair a phone, then scan the QR
  code with Sevbify on your phone. Send songs, albums and folders either way. It only works on your own Wi-Fi,
  nothing goes through the internet, everything is encrypted, and you say yes to each transfer.
- **Instant zoom:** Ctrl + / Ctrl − / Ctrl 0 change the size straight away.
- **Equaliser fixed:** each slider now changes the frequency it shows.
- **Windows taskbar buttons:** hover over Sevbify in the taskbar for Like, Previous, Play/Pause and Next.
- **Video podcasts keep playing in a small floating window** when you leave the video page.
- **Scroll through previews:** flick through a playlist one song at a time (a playlist's ••• menu).
- **More artists:** artists found on SoundCloud get their own page with their albums.
- **Made for you** mixes and **top songs in your country** on Home, worked out on your device from what you play.
- **Smooth scrolling**, **Help → FAQ** (F1), playlist descriptions, and many smaller fixes.
- **Android:** the Your Library grid shows everything as tiles. **iPhone:** a slimmer progress bar and a tidier Now
  Playing screen.

Tested automatically on Windows 10 and 11, macOS, Ubuntu 22.04 and 24.04, an Android emulator and an iPhone
simulator.

## Install

**Windows**
1. Under **Assets** below, download **Sevbify-Windows.zip**, right-click it → **Extract All…** → **Extract**.
2. Open the `Sevbify` folder and double-click **Sevbify.exe**. If Windows says "Windows protected your PC", click
   **More info** → **Run anyway** (the app isn't signed yet).
3. Optional: Settings → **Add to Desktop and Start menu**.

**macOS** (Apple Silicon)
1. Download **Sevbify-macOS.zip**, unzip it and drag **Sevbify** into **Applications**.
2. The first time, right-click Sevbify → **Open** → **Open** (or System Settings → Privacy & Security → Open Anyway).

**Linux** (64-bit, Ubuntu 22.04 or newer, or similar)
1. Install VLC: `sudo apt install vlc` (Fedora: `sudo dnf install vlc`, Arch: `sudo pacman -S vlc`).
2. Download **Sevbify-Linux.tar.gz**, extract it, and run `./Sevbify` in the `Sevbify` folder. `./install.sh` adds
   it to your app menu.

**Android**
1. On your phone, download **Sevbify-Android.apk** from Assets and open it. Allow your browser to install apps if
   asked, then tap **Install** (if Play Protect warns about an unknown app, tap **Install anyway**).
2. Open Sevbify and allow **Music and audio** and **Notifications**.

**iPhone / iPad** (iOS 16 or newer, free with SideStore or AltStore)
1. Install SideStore (sidestore.io) or AltStore (altstore.io).
2. On your iPhone, download **Sevbify-iOS.ipa** from Assets in Safari, then in SideStore / AltStore: **My Apps → +**
   and choose it. Turn on Developer Mode if iOS asks.
3. For updates: Sevbify's Settings → Sevbify updates → **Add Sevbify to SideStore**.
4. With a free Apple ID, SideStore / AltStore refreshes the app every 7 days.

After installing, Sevbify offers new versions itself. Your playlists, settings and music are kept.

The first time you pair a phone, your computer may ask whether Sevbify may use the network (Windows firewall,
macOS), and your iPhone asks about devices on your local network: choose **Allow**.

## Please use it responsibly

Only download music you have the right to download, for example songs you've bought, that the artist offers for
free, or that are openly licensed. Sevbify isn't affiliated with any music service it works with.
